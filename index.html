Services What We Do

Custom Card Design

We want to make the perfect card for you.

If you can imagine it, we will do the work of making it real! Our primary goal is to help you communicate from your heart, with the perfect handmade card and/or gift box. We will personally work with you, to create the design. Then we will put it all together and give you a chance to review the result. If there are any changes we will make them. When you are satisfied that your card (or box) is perfect, we will deliver it to you with a handmade envelope that is just the right size. Pricing will vary depending on the design.

About Who we are

About Us

We are Glenn and Cristin Dewar. We have been married for eightteen years, and have been blessed with twelve wonderful children. Glenn takes care of the website and business aspects while giving input on designs and occasionally helping with the production process. Cristin designs and constructs our products, while giving input on the look of the website. Together we show and sell Cristin's work at Artist In Residence, craft fairs and markets.

We highly value living with integrity, doing everything with excellence, and investing in our family. We believe God is the creative force in the universe and that He desires to express that creativity through us.

Cristin's Artist Bio

Paper artist Cristin Dewar was born and raised in New England, with years spent traveling through the South Pacific islands of Hawaii and New Zealand, where she met and married her husband and gave birth to the first of their twelve children. Cristin and family currently reside in northern Vermont, where she home schools her children, in addition to creating her designs.
The chaos of daily life fuels the versatility in Cristin's work, ranging from hand-stamped greeting cards to unique gift boxes; each practical item a communication from the heart.
While inspired by the work of other artists, Cristin's pieces reflect modern-day designs with crisp lines and multi-dimensional layers. The artist began selling her art in 2015 at the "beCAUSE" and "Autumn on the Green" craft fairs; while her work can now be viewed in person at "Artist in Residence;" a cooperative art gallery in St. Albans, VT;

Credit where it is due

All of the completed designs are ours, but many of them are based off and/or inspired by cards made by other people. Thank you to the many artists whom we have learned from in person and other media. Your completed cards, techniques, and included elements have inspired us greatly. Thank you also to our family and friends who's feedback and support is greatly appreciated.
